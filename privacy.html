Privacy

Short version: Rewound collects nothing about you and sends nothing to the developer.

000In short

Rewound does not collect personal data. There is no account or sign-in, no ads, no analytics or tracking, and no third-party SDK. Nothing is sent to the developer.

001Microphone

Rewound listens only while you have tapped the record button and a recording is running. iOS asks for your permission the first time you record. If you said no, you can allow it later in the iPhone’s Settings › Rewound › Microphone.

002What is stored, and where

Your recordings, saved game rounds, player names and settings are stored only on your device, in the app’s own storage. The settings are the countdown, the vibration, the number of players, whether Pro has been bought, and a counter that decides when the app may ask for a rating.

If backup is on, this data can end up in your own device backup (iCloud or your computer), like the data of any app. Rewound has no access to that backup.

003Photos and Files

Importing uses the system’s own pickers. For a video from Photos, Rewound receives only the video you pick and takes its sound; it does not need permission to access your photo library. For Files, it receives only the file you choose.

004Sharing a recording

A recording leaves your device only when you export it yourself through the share sheet. You decide where it goes.

005Purchases

Rewound Pro is bought through Apple (App Store). The developer never sees your payment details. The app asks Apple, on your device, whether Pro has been bought. The only requests that leave the device are the App Store’s own purchase requests.

006Rating request

If the app asks you to rate it, that is the standard iOS window. The developer does not see who rated or what they wrote.

007Deleting your data

Delete items one by one in the Library, everything at once with Settings › Delete all recordings, or delete the app, which removes all of it.

008Children

Rewound collects data from no one, so it collects none from children either.

009Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published on this page, with the date. It was last updated on 25 November 2026.

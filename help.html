Help

Rewound records a sound and plays it backwards. Then it teaches you to say it backwards yourself.

000What Rewound does

Record any sound and hear it played in reverse. The game takes it one step further: you say a short phrase, listen to it backwards, and try to copy that backwards sound with your own voice. Rewound flips your attempt back around and tells you how close it came.

001Recording

  • Open the Record tab and tap the big round button. Say something, then tap it again to stop.
  • The first time, iOS asks whether Rewound may use the microphone. Say yes, otherwise it cannot record.
  • If you like, turn on Countdown before recording in Settings (the gear icon); it counts 3, 2, 1 before recording starts.
  • A recording opens with Reversed selected. Tap the play button to hear it backwards, or choose Forward to hear it as you said it. Tap or drag across the waveform to jump to any spot.

002The game on your own

Open the Game tab and keep Solo selected.

  1. Say a short phrase, then listen to it backwards.
  2. Under the player the phrase is cut into numbered pieces, 2 to 6 of them, split at its quiet moments. Tap a piece and it plays in a loop. Switch on Slow to hear it at 0.6× speed.
  3. Tap Copy it and record yourself copying the backwards sound.
  4. Rewound turns your attempt back around and gives it a score out of 100. Each piece gets a mark and its number: ✓ matched, = close, ✗ off. It names the piece that strayed the most, and Loop piece N (with that piece’s number) takes you straight back to it.
  5. Save round puts the round in your Library.

You can try the same phrase up to 10 times. After that, start a new one.

003Playing with friends

On the Game tab choose Pass the phone and set the number of players, from 2 to 6. Tap a player to type a name; Rewound remembers the names.

One of you records the phrase, then everyone copies it in turn and passes the phone on. At the end you get a table that starts with the closest attempt, and every attempt can be played back flipped around again.

Same phrase again starts another round with the same phrase. Save round keeps the round in the Library.

004A phrase to play with

A recording of up to 15 seconds can become the game’s phrase: open it and tap Copy it in the game. A saved round has Play this phrase again.

Nothing to say? Under Stuck for a phrase? you find a suggestion, and Another shows the next one. They are only ideas; say whatever you like.

005How the score works

Rewound compares the original phrase with your attempt once it has been flipped back around. It looks at rhythm (how loud each moment is) and brightness (hissy sounds versus vowels). All of this happens on your iPhone.

Saying it a little faster or slower is not punished. The score is for fun, not a measurement of how well you sing, and it is not speech recognition: Rewound does not understand words, it only compares how the two sounds are shaped. The marks under the pieces come from the same comparison.

006Library, vibration and deleting

  • Your recordings and saved rounds live in the Library tab. Swipe a row to delete it; touch and hold to rename it. The free version keeps 5 items.
  • Settings › Delete all recordings removes everything at once.
  • To switch the vibration off, go to Settings › Vibration feedback.

007Something not working?

The record button does nothing, or it says “Microphone is off”

Rewound is not allowed to use the microphone. Open the iPhone’s Settings › Rewound and switch Microphone on. The microphone is only used while you are recording.

My recording stopped after 15 seconds

That is the limit of the free version. Pro records up to 5 minutes.

I bought Pro but it is still locked

Tap Restore Purchase on the Pro screen, below the buy button, or in Settings (the gear icon). Use the same Apple Account you bought with.

008Rewound Pro

Pro is a single purchase. There is no subscription. The game, its pieces and the pass-the-phone rounds stay free. Pro adds:

  • recordings of up to 5 minutes (free: 15 seconds)
  • an unlimited library (free: 5 items)
  • importing sound: Audio from Files and Sound of a video (a video from Photos; Rewound takes its sound)
  • speed from 0.5× to 2× and pitch up to 12 semitones up or down, under Effects
  • Loop, which repeats the stretch between the two handles on the waveform, and Trim, which keeps only that stretch
  • export as m4a (small file) or wav (lossless) with the share button

Only import sounds you have the right to use.
